Every platform has its own username rules, and they disagree on almost everything: how long a name can be, which symbols are allowed and how often you can change it. A name that works on TikTok can be rejected by Roblox, and a Discord name can lose its capital letters. This guide puts the rules side by side so you can pick a name that fits where you want to use it.

We checked every rule on the platform’s own help pages or sign-up checks on 19 November 2026. Where a platform does not publish a rule, the table says so instead of guessing.

Username rules at a glance

Discord username rules

Discord usernames are 2 to 32 characters and can only use lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and periods. Discord forces every username to lowercase, so NeonFox becomes neonfox, and it does not allow two periods in a row. Every username is unique, and you can change yours up to 2 times a week.

Your display name is separate. It can be 1 to 32 characters, can include spaces, symbols and emoji, and can be changed at any time, so that is the place for capital letters and style. Our Discord username generator only makes names that already fit these rules.

Roblox username rules

A Roblox username must be 3 to 20 characters long and can use letters, numbers and at most one underscore, which can’t be the first or last character. Spaces, periods and other symbols are rejected. We tested each rule against Roblox’s own sign-up check, and every rejection comes with a short message, such as “Usernames can have at most one _”.

Usernames are unique, and each change costs Robux, while your display name can be changed for free once every seven days. The Roblox username generator shows each name’s length out of 20 and has a short guide to fixing a rejected name.

Xbox gamertag rules

Xbox support says a gamertag can be up to 15 characters long, including spaces, and can’t start with a number. Gamertags of 13 characters or more must be unique and use only Latin letters and numbers. Shorter gamertags don’t have to be unique: if someone already has your name, Xbox adds a number suffix after a # sign, such as NovaFlux#482. Xbox’s developer documentation confirms the suffix system and a 12-character limit for the name part of these suffixed gamertags.

Your first gamertag change is free, even if Xbox picked the gamertag for you at sign-up. After that, every change costs a fee that varies by region. Try the gamertag generator for Xbox-friendly names.

TikTok username rules

A TikTok username must be 2 to 24 characters and can contain letters, numbers, underscores and periods, but it can’t end with a period. You can change it once every 30 days, and changing it also changes your profile link. If you give up a username, you can’t take it back for a short period, and names held by suspended or deactivated accounts may not be available straight away.

Your TikTok nickname is the name people see across the app and is separate from the username. For handle ideas, start with the username generator.

PlayStation online ID rules

PlayStation’s help pages explain how to change an online ID but do not list its length or character rules; those are only shown on the sign-up form. What PlayStation does publish: your first change is free and later changes cost a fee, you can change it as many times as you want, and if you run into technical problems after a change you can switch back to your original online ID for free. Child accounts can’t change their online ID, and an ID that breaks the terms of service is replaced with one starting with “temp-”.

Changing your online ID signs you out of all devices, and PlayStation warns that some PS4 games may have problems after a change, so pick carefully the first time. The gamertag generator works for PlayStation names too.

Instagram username rules

Instagram’s help center does not publish a length limit or a full character list for usernames. It does suggest adding periods, numbers, underscores or abbreviations when the name you want is taken, which tells you those characters are accepted. You can usually change back to a username you used before, as long as no one else has taken it in the meantime.

If your account reaches a lot of people, a username change may be reviewed before it goes live, and your followers may be told about the change. For softer, style-led handles, try the aesthetic username generator.

A username that fits almost everywhere

If you want the same name on several platforms, stay inside the rules they share. Based on the rules above, a name of 3 to 15 letters and numbers that starts with a letter and has no spaces or symbols fits the published character rules on Discord, Roblox, Xbox and TikTok. Discord will show it in lowercase, so pick something that still reads well that way. On Xbox, a name of 13 or more characters must not already be taken, while a shorter one can be shared and gets a # number after it.

  • 3 to 15 characters
  • Starts with a letter
  • Letters and numbers only: no spaces, periods or underscores
  • Still easy to read in all lowercase

Fitting the rules is only half the job. The name also has to be free on each platform, and none of these sites share a waiting list, so check them one by one before you settle on it.
